Ferry timetable changes on Monday

Two extra morning sailings join the timetable next week, leaving the old quay at ten past seven and twenty to eight. The last return sailing stays at half past nine.

Tickets bought on board cost a third more than tickets bought at the kiosk, so arrive a few minutes early. Bicycles travel free outside the morning rush.

In rough weather the crossing takes forty minutes instead of twenty-five. Check the board at the quay before you set out.
