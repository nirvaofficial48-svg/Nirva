How a ring resonator traps light

Light travelling along a straight waveguide can leak into a ring placed beside it. It stays there only when the ring's circumference fits a whole number of wavelengths.

At those wavelengths each lap adds in step with the last, and the light builds up inside the ring. Read how that makes a filter.
